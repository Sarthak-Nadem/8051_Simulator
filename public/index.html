⌘
01 · Basics

What is the 8051?

An 8-bit microcontroller family used to teach embedded systems. A typical classic 8051 includes CPU registers, program and data memory, timers, serial communication and I/O ports.

▦
02 · Registers

Registers & flags

A is the accumulator; R0–R7 are the active register-bank registers. PC tracks program execution. PSW contains status flags including carry, auxiliary carry, overflow and parity.

⇄
03 · Addressing

Addressing modes

Immediate: MOV A,#25H. Register: MOV A,R0. Direct: MOV A,30H. These examples look similar but obtain data from different places.

∑
04 · Arithmetic

Arithmetic operations

ADD adds, SUBB subtracts with borrow, MUL AB multiplies, and DIV AB gives a quotient and remainder. Try all four and watch the flags.

◈
05 · Logic

Logical operations

ANL is bitwise AND, ORL is bitwise OR, and XRL is XOR. CLR clears a bit/byte and CPL complements it.

↪
06 · Flow

Branching & control

Instructions such as SJMP, JZ, JNZ and DJNZ change execution flow. Try labels and a loop in the simulator to see control flow in action.
